# Chapter 1 — Introduction

## 1.1 Overview of Vehicle Service Management

Modern automotive workshops, multi-brand service stations, and dealership service centers manage high volumes of vehicle maintenance, diagnostic work, repair order dispatching, spare parts inventory, and customer invoicing. 

Traditional workshop management often suffers from fragmented paper job cards, manual phone scheduling, uncoordinated inventory tracking, delayed customer updates, and invoicing errors.

## 1.2 Problems with Manual Workshop Management

1. **Scheduling Bottlenecks**: Overbooking or idle technician bays due to manual calendar tracking.
2. **Inventory Stockouts**: Spare parts running out mid-service due to lack of automatic stock deduction and minimum stock alerts.
3. **Status Opacity**: Customers unable to track repair progress without repeatedly calling service advisors.
4. **Billing Inaccuracies**: Discrepancies between parts installed on the shop floor and billed line items.

## 1.3 The Need for GarageFlow

**GarageFlow** (**Smart Vehicle Service Management**) solves these operational friction points by delivering a centralized, web-based platform with role-based access control for both Customers and Admin/Service Center Staff.

## 1.4 Objectives & Scope

- **Customer Empowerment**: Self-service vehicle registration, online service appointment booking, real-time status tracking, and digital printable invoices.
- **Workshop Telemetry**: Executive Operations Dashboard displaying real-time bay count, pending appointments, revenue, and low-stock alerts.
- **Inventory Automation**: Automated stock deduction upon service completion with non-negative stock enforcement.
- **Quality Assurance**: Comprehensive 60+ test case execution, REST API Postman collection, defect logging, and cross-browser testing.
